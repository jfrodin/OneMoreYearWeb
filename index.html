ONE MORE YEAR

Every life is a part of history.

Join the playtest Coming to Steam

A family. A century. One year at a time.

You play one person, one year at a time, from the cradle to the grave. You go to school, fall in love, find work, lose it, have children or don't, and every year something happens that asks what you will do.

When your life ends, the story goes on. You carry on as someone you leave behind: a child, a sibling, a niece. They inherit your money, your face, your temper and your secrets, and the family remembers what you did.

The family is the real hero. See how far it goes.

THE FAMILY CHRONICLE

1950 onwards  ·  Sweden, Britain, Germany and America  ·  Every year

Your family lives in real history

Wages, prices, laws, wars and the news are those of the year you are in. A Swede queues at the state liquor shop on a Friday; a Briton hears last orders at ten to eleven; a family in Leipzig waits fourteen years for a car, and wakes up one November morning to find the Wall open. Your first family in a country begins in the 1950s and lives it all.

Sweden

1967. At ten to five this morning every car in Sweden stopped, crossed to the other side of the road, and waited.

1974. ABBA win the Eurovision Song Contest in Brighton.

1992. The krona collapses. Interest rates reach five hundred percent.

Britain

1966. England win the World Cup at Wembley. Strangers hug in the street.

1984. The miners go on strike, and stay out for a year.

2016. The country votes to leave the European Union, by a whisker.

Germany

1961. On the thirteenth of August a wall goes up through Berlin. Families wake up divided.

1989. In Leipzig seventy thousand march around the ring road. The police do not shoot.

1990. The West mark comes to the East. Queues outside the banks from five in the morning.

America

1963. President Kennedy is shot dead in Dallas.

1969. A man walks on the Moon, and the whole family is up to see it.

2001. A Tuesday morning in September that nobody forgets.

What you do comes back

Choices lead somewhere

Steal once, and the fence finds you. Have one affair, and the next is easier. Drink alone, and one morning your hands shake. A sober, faithful, law abiding life never meets those moments at all.

People remember

Every person in the family has their own memories of you, and their own opinion. Children grow up with what you gave them, and with what you didn't.

Life as it is

Love, work, money, children, illness and grief, and also drink, drugs, affairs and crime. Nothing is switched off; you leave it out by not choosing it.

Close to 1,500 moments

From a first word to a last wish, and the history of four countries, decade by decade. Each generation reads the same moments in its own words.
