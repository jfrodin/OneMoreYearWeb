Coming to Steam

What would you do with one more year?

You get one life, a year at a time, from the cradle to the grave. Every year asks you something. What you answer is up to you.

  • 1961 · East BerlinGrow up with the Wall outside your bedroom window. Climb it one night, or stay and see if it ever falls.
  • 1971 · GothenburgMarry your school sweetheart at twenty. Or run off with the neighbour, and explain it to the children later.
  • 1984 · BarnsleyStand on the picket line with the miners for a whole year. Or go back to work, and live with what the street calls you.
  • 1997 · SeattlePut every penny you have into a little software company. Sell before the crash, if you can bear to.
  • Any year · AnywhereSteal a car at seventeen. Find out who comes knocking at thirty.
  • 1994 · ManchesterWin the lottery at sixty, and learn which of your children start calling more often.

And when your life ends, you keep going: as your son, your niece, your granddaughter. They inherit your money, your face, your temper and your secrets. The family remembers what you did.

How far can you take one family?

THE FAMILY CHRONICLE

From 1950  ·  Sweden, Britain, Germany and America  ·  Every year a new edition

History happens to you

You live in it. The news, the prices, the laws, the music and the wars are those of your year, in the country you were born in. Your grandmother queues with a ration book; your grandson does his shopping on a phone. And somewhere in between, your family is there when it all happens.

Sweden

Summer cottages, midsummer schnapps and the people's home.

1967. At ten to five in the morning every car in Sweden stops, crosses to the other side of the road, and waits.

1974. ABBA win Eurovision with Waterloo. The whole street is singing it by Monday.

Britain

Council estates, the pools, punk and last orders.

1966. England win the World Cup at Wembley. Strangers hug in the street.

1988. A field, a sound system and five thousand people dancing until the sun comes up.

Germany

Born East or West of a wall, and everything that follows.

1961. On the thirteenth of August a wall goes up through Berlin. Families wake up divided.

1989. The ninth of November. Strangers hand out sparkling wine at the border.

America

The draft, the suburbs, the Moon and the dream.

1969. A man walks on the Moon, and the whole family stays up to see it.

1985. Las Vegas, a hot table, and the feeling at three in the morning that the next hand is the one.

Every choice leaves a mark

Choices with a memory

Place one bet and the bookie learns your name. Have one affair and the next one finds you. Keep your nose clean and other doors open: a promotion, an old friend, a house full of people at Christmas.

A family that remembers

Everyone has their own memories of you. Your children grow up with what you gave them, and your grandchildren hear the stories, some of them true.

All of life

Love, work, money, children and grief, and booze, bets, crime and second chances. It is all in there, waiting for the day you go looking.

Close to 1,500 moments

From a first word to a last wish, and seventy years of history in four countries. Each generation meets them in its own words.
